Photography Portfolio Website Plan

Site Name

CRC Photography

Site Purpose

A portfolio website to showcase my photography work, provide information about me, and offer a way for potential clients to contact me.

Scenarios

  • A visitor wants to browse my photo gallery.
  • A potential client wants to learn more about me and my photography style.
  • Someone wants to contact me for a photoshoot or collaboration.

Color Scheme

Primary: #222 (Dark Gray/Black)
Secondary: #f4f4f4 (Light Gray/White)
Accent: #ff6b6b (Soft Red)

Typography

Heading Font Montserrat
Body Font: Open Sans
